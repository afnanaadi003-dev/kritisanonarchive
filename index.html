Biography & Trajectory

Engineering Precision Meets Pure Cinematic Emotion

The Outsider's Triumph

Born in New Delhi to a professor mother and chartered accountant father, Kriti Sanon earned her Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering. Driven by artistic passion, she transitioned into modeling and made her screen debut in the Telugu psychological thriller 1: Nenokkadine (2014).

Her Hindi debut in Heropanti earned her the Filmfare Award for Best Female Debut. However, it was her portrayal of the rebellious, small-town girl Bitti Mishra in Bareilly Ki Barfi (2017) that shifted industry perception, establishing her as an actress of immense depth and comedic charm.

The defining zenith came with Mimi (2021), where Kriti gained 15 kilograms to portray an unwed surrogate mother. Her performance won her the 69th National Film Award for Best Actress. In 2023, she launched her production banner Blue Butterfly Films, producing and starring in the thriller Do Patti, while continuing to dominate the box office with commercial hits like Crew and Teri Baaton Mein Aisa Ujha Jiya.

"Being an outsider gives you an edge—you don't come in with predefined expectations, and every single step forward is entirely your own victory."

Production House Vision

Taking the Driver's Seat

Established in 2023, Blue Butterfly Films represents Kriti Sanon's transition from performer to producer. Fueled by a desire to bring gripping, performance-driven scripts to life without waiting for opportunities to come to her.

  • Do Patti (2024) — Maiden production venture starring Kajol and Kriti in dual roles.
  • Focus on narrative-driven thrillers, complex female relationships, and fresh writer voices.
  • Creating sustainable opportunities for women in writing, directing, and production management.
Entrepreneurship & Style

Hyphen Beauty & Ms. Taken

Beyond cinema, Kriti is a serial entrepreneur. She co-founded Hyphen, a skincare brand prioritizing clean formulations, and her apparel brand Ms. Taken, catering to modern, independent young women.

"Starting Hyphen and Blue Butterfly Films came from the same impulse: taking charge of my own creative and business narrative."
